# Shipped criteria

The author's three criteria, written from the top modes of the shipped taxonomy (see `datasets/traces/author_taxonomy.json`). Module 5 grades the first one. Your own criteria are in `v1.md`; the shipped ones, not yours, are used later, so an unusual choice never breaks module 5.

## 1. States the refund policy correctly
Applies to: replies to refund tickets only
Question: Does the reply state FreshMarket's refund policy correctly?
Pass example: "Missing items are refunded in full with no return needed, back to your original payment method within 5 working days."
Fail example: "I have refunded your whole order as a goodwill gesture, and the money will be back within 24 hours."

## 2. Avoids claiming an action it cannot take
Applies to: every reply
Question: Does the reply avoid saying it is checking, escalating or fixing something outside the food safety hand-off?
Pass example: "I can't see the tracking from here, so please send the delivery date and a photo and a team member will read them."
Fail example: "I'm also checking why your earlier request wasn't handled and I will get it fixed for you today."

## 3. Keeps to what the ticket and policy support
Applies to: every reply
Question: Does the reply avoid describing how the app or the account works in a way neither the ticket nor the policy gives?
Pass example: "Account changes such as your email are made by you in the app, and support can explain how."
Fail example: "Only the newest reset link works, so please delete the older emails from your inbox first."
