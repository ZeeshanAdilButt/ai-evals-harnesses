# FreshMarket support policy (short form)

- Refund window: 7 days from delivery for any item, with a photo for damaged or spoiled food.
- Missing items: refunded in full, no return needed.
- Late delivery (more than 2 hours late): delivery fee refunded.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days.
- Account changes (email, address, password) are done by the customer in the app; support can explain how.
- Food safety concerns (allergens, spoiled or contaminated food) are urgent and go to a person the same day.
