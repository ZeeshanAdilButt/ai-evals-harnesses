# FreshMarket support policy (short form)

- Refund window: 14 days from delivery for any item.
- Missing items: refunded as FreshMarket store credit, no return needed.
- Late delivery (more than 1 hour late): delivery fee refunded.
- Refunds arrive within 2 working days.
- Account changes (email, address, password) are done by the customer in the app; support can explain how.
- Food safety concerns (allergens, spoiled or contaminated food) go to a person the same day.
